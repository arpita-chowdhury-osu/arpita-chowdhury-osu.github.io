🏫 I am a <b>Ph.D. Candidate</b> in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at <b>[The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu/)</b>, advised by <b>[Wei-Lun (Harry) Chao](https://sites.google.com/view/wei-lun-harry-chao)</b>. 

My research interests lie in <b>machine learning</b> and <b>vision-language domain</b>. Specifically,
- Long Video Understanding and Reasoning
- Multimodal Learning
- Interpretable/ Explainable AI

Previously, I received my M.Sc. degree in CSE from <b>[The Ohio State University](https://www.osu.edu/)</b>. I also recieved B.Sc. degree in Software Engineering from IIT in [University of Dhaka](http://www.iit.du.ac.bd/). 

Professionally, I had a wonderful time working as research intern in <b>Qualcomm</b>, USA (2025) and a backend engineer in <b>[Samsung R&D Institute](https://research.samsung.com/srbd)</b>
